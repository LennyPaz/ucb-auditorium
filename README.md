# UCB Welcome Center Auditorium: AV Concept Model

An interactive 3D concept of the planned AV refresh for the Welcome Center auditorium (University Center Building B, room B1401) at Florida State University.

**View it:** https://lennypaz.github.io/ucb-auditorium/

## What it shows

- Two Newline DV One+ 135" all-in-one LED walls on the front wall (projection screen and whiteboards removed)
- Flown loudspeakers on each side, main on top with its subwoofer below
- The presenter console, sized after the one in the room today
- The seating as drawn on the FSU space plan: a short front row and wheelchair spaces, six curved rows, and a back row along the curved wall

## Using it

- **Overview** and **Top down** orbit around the room. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom.
- **Row 3 seat**, **Back row**, **Front row**, and **Presenter** put you at eye level. Drag to look around.
- Click any seated person to see the room from their seat.
- **Hide panel** collapses the settings so the model fills the screen.

## Sources and assumptions

- Room plan: FSU Facilities space drawing for UCB, first floor (room B1401, 1,334 sq ft). Dimensions are scaled from the drawing and are approximate.
- Ceiling heights, riser heights, and the row 3 level change are from a site visit.
- Video wall size: Newline DV One+ 135" (DV-13524-PLUS) dimensional drawing.
- The final console model and size are not known yet.

This is a concept for discussion, not a construction drawing.
